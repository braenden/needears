Last updated: <LegalUpdated />

These are the rules for using Need Ears, a free service. By creating an account or uploading, you agree to these terms.

## Who can use Need Ears

You must be at least 13 years old. Anyone can listen without an account. You need an account to upload or comment.

## Your music is yours

- You upload only music you made yourself, or that you have the right to share. You confirm this each time you upload.
- You keep all rights to your music and your comments. We own nothing of what you post.
- AI-generated music is not allowed. You confirm that your track is not generated by AI when you upload.

## What you let Need Ears do with it

When you upload a track or write a comment, you give Need Ears a non-exclusive license to store it, convert it (for example into a format that streams well, and to draw the waveform), show it and stream it, only so that the service can work. This license ends when the track or comment is deleted, by you, the group's owner or us, or when your account is deleted. A copy may remain in provider backups for a short time after that.

Tracks in the feed are public. Anyone with the link, signed in or not, can listen. Tracks in a group are for its members (see Groups below). Need Ears does not offer downloads, but we can't prevent someone from recording what they hear, in the feed or in a group. Don't upload anything you aren't comfortable with being heard by everyone who can listen to it.

Need Ears does not keep your original file, only the converted stream. Keep your own originals.

## Groups

- In a group, only its members hear the tracks and read the comments. Anyone can find a group by its name and see who owns it and how many are in it.
- The person who makes a group owns it. The owner decides who is in, and can remove tracks, comments and members. If you post in a group, its owner can delete what you posted.
- These terms and the [community guidelines](/guidelines) apply in groups too, and you can report tracks and comments there the same way. We can still hide or remove content and accounts in a group, as anywhere else. To handle a report or a request from the police or a court, we can look at what is in a group, and every time is logged.
- Group names are public, so a name must follow the guidelines too.
- If you leave a group, or its owner removes you, your tracks stay in it. You can delete them in Settings. If the owner deletes the group, or their account, every track in it is deleted.

## What we can do

- We can hide or remove tracks, comments and accounts that break these terms or the [community guidelines](/guidelines).
- We don't scan uploads automatically, so we rely on reports. We can remove a track if we reasonably believe it is AI-generated. If you think we're wrong, email <ContactEmail />, and we'll take another look.
