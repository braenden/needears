# Privacy policy

Last updated: <PrivacyUpdated />

This page says what Need Ears stores about you, why, where it lives, and how you can see it, fix it or delete it.

## Who is responsible

The data controller is Kevin Brenden. Contact: <ContactEmail />.

## What we store

| What                   | Details                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Account                | Your email address (from Google or from the email link), and a sign-in session (with the IP address and browser it came from)                                                                                                              |
| Profile                | Username, display name, avatar, bio, what you're working on, your DAW and your links. If you sign in with Google, the name and picture Google provides may be used as a starting point                                                     |
| Tracks                 | The audio (converted for streaming), its waveform, and title, genre, tags, description, what you want feedback on, and your experience level                                                                                               |
| Comments and reactions | Your comments, reactions, and which comments were marked Helpful                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Groups                 | The groups you own and the groups you're in, and when you joined. Who asked to join a group you own, and the groups you asked to join. If a group's owner removed you, we remember that until the group is deleted, so you can't ask again |
| Reports                | Reports you file, and reports about your content                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Notifications          | In-app notifications                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| IP address at upload   | The IP address you uploaded a track from, stored for 30 days, then deleted automatically                                                                                                                                                   |
| Plays                  | When you have listened to a track for 30 seconds, that it was played: by your account if you're signed in, otherwise by a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address. Stored for 2 days, then deleted automatically                        |

We do not store your original audio file, only the converted stream.

## Why

- To run your account and show your profile, tracks and comments (needed to provide the service you signed up for).
- To keep the service safe: the IP address at upload is kept for 30 days so that copyright complaints and abuse can be traced, and reports are used for moderation. This is our legitimate interest in keeping the site safe and lawful.
- To count plays honestly: a play counts once per listener, so the numbers can't be pumped up. This is our legitimate interest in showing numbers people can trust.
- To keep the site working: error reports when something breaks.

We don't sell data, and we don't show ads.

## What is public

Your profile (username, display name, avatar, bio, what you're working on, your DAW, your links, reputation and when you joined), your tracks in the feed and your comments on them are public. Anyone can see them without an account. Your email address is not shown to other users.

Groups are different. Anyone can find a group by its name and see who owns it and how many are in it. Only its members see the tracks, the comments and who is in it. Tracks in a group are not in the feed, in search or on your profile.

## Groups

- Only the members of a group hear its tracks and read the comments on them.
- We look at what is in a group only to handle a report about it, or a request from the police or a court. Every time is logged, and we keep that log for 12 months.
- If you leave a group, or its owner removes you, the tracks you posted there stay in the group. You can delete them under Your tracks in Settings.
- The owner of a group can delete tracks and comments in it.
- Deleting a group, or the account of the person who owns it, deletes every track in it, for everyone.

Our backups are encrypted before they leave our hands, and only we have the key.

## Cookies

We use only the strictly necessary cookie that keeps you signed in, and your light or dark theme choice is saved in your browser. We don't load third-party scripts that set cookies. That's why there is no cookie banner.

## How long we keep it

- Your account, tracks and comments: until you delete them or your account.
- IP address at upload: 30 days.
- Plays (who listened to which track): 2 days.
- Your previous usernames: 90 days, so old links to your profile keep working.
- Groups: until you leave or the group is deleted. If the owner says no when you ask to join, we keep that for 7 days, so you can't ask again right away.
- The log of when we opened something in a group: 12 months.
- Reports: kept after the reported track, comment or account is deleted, as a record of what we did. The copy of the reported text is kept for 12 months after we decide, or 12 months after the report if we never decide. For something in a group, the copy goes when it is deleted.
- Backups: up to 90 days. After you delete something, it can remain in an encrypted backup for up to 90 days before it is gone.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, fix, move or delete your data:

- **See and take your data:** Settings, "Download my data". You get a file with all of it, including your groups and the groups you asked to join.
- **Fix it:** edit your profile in Settings, or email us.
- **Delete it:** Settings, "Delete account". It removes your account, your tracks and your comments, also in groups. Groups you own are deleted too, with every track in them. Reports about your content stay as a record, as described above. You'll be asked to sign in again first.
- **Anything else** (for example objecting to processing, or restricting it): email <ContactEmail />. We'll answer within 30 days.

## Complaints

If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, [Datatilsynet](https://www.datatilsynet.no). We'd appreciate hearing from you first, so we can fix it.
