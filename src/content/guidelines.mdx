# Community guidelines

Short version: be specific, be kind, talk about the track and not the person.

1. **Be specific.** "The kick and bass fight around 60 Hz at 0:48" helps. "Sounds bad" doesn't. If you only have a feeling, say that: "I lost interest here" is useful too.
2. **Be kind.** Honest is good. Cruel is not. Everyone here made something and shared it to make it better.
3. **Critique the track, not the person.** "The second verse drags" is fine. Comments about someone's age, gender, country or taste in music are not.
4. **Only your own music.** Upload only tracks you made yourself and have the right to share. Don't upload other people's work.
5. **No AI-generated music.** Tracks generated by AI tools (Suno, Udio and the like) are not allowed here.
6. **No ads.** Don't use comments or tracks to sell things, spam links or promote services.
7. **No hate.** No harassment, threats, slurs or discrimination. Don't share anyone's personal details.

## Reporting

See something that breaks these rules? Use the report button on a track or comment. Reported tracks and comments can be hidden until we've looked at them.

## What happens if you break them

Depending on how serious it is and whether it keeps happening: your content is hidden or removed, or your account is limited or banned. For copyright problems, see the [copyright page](/copyright).

If you think we got it wrong, email <ContactEmail /> and we'll take another look.
